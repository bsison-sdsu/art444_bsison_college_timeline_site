SWC SEMESTER 1

FALL 2023

ART 100/GEOL100/GEOL101/PHIL12O

In the fall 2023 I went back to school after taking a year and half break. Coming back I fully transitioned from be a computer science major to becoming a graphic design student. In my first semester back I started picking at my GE’s with geology and philosophy. I also started my first major prep class which was drawing 1. These classes were nice way to get back into the rhythm of the college environment.

SWC SEMESTER 2

SPRING 2024

ART 101/ART 131/ART161/ART192/SOC115

This semester I decided to get a lot more of my major prop classes out of the way, finishing art electives, more GE requirements, and taking my first graphic design class. In the graphic design class I took we got more into of the branding side of the industry and learning illustrator. In the class I was able to make my first logo for a fake design studio ass my last project. Other class I took explored other mediums of art like film and another introduced me to a lot of Filipino American history. The Fil-Am history taught me a lot about my own heritage and opened my eyes to what it means to be Filipino.

SWC SEMESTER 3

SUMMER 2024

ART 151

For this summer semester I really wanted to make sure that I was on track to transfer as soon as possible so I decided to take an art history class to fulfill one of my major prop requirements. This course covered a bit about early Renaissance art and followed art history all the way up to modern art. My favorite part of art history we learned about in this course was the Pop art era as this movement is what I related to the most and thought looked most appealing.

SWC SEMESTER 4

FALL 2024

ART 103/ART160/ART168/CIS123

By this semester I was pretty much done with most of my major prep requirements and took the graphic design portfolio class. This class was very challenging as we always had at least two different projects that were due at the end of each month. While challenging I feel it was a great class to really push my design abilities. Other classes I took dealt with other art requirements, getting me into other mediums lik sculpting and since I still had room I took a computer information systems class. This class just taught the basics of web design and backend management data-management through php. I liked this class a lot as I still loved coding even after leaving compsci and the class was a joy to be in.

SWC SEMESTER 5

SPRING 2025

ART 102/ART170

With this being the last semester I had at Southwestern community college I only had two more art class to fulfill. I had to take ceramics and drawing 2. Both of these classes challenged me in art mediums I don’t really enjoy. Drawing 2 introduced color in drawings and getting shading and colors right was a big challenge for me. In the end I did find small improvements in my drawing abilities. For ceramics, it was tough learning all the techniques in molding clay and all the dirt and water we interacted with when doing assignments left me all dirty by the end of class. In the end, I was able to get a hang of things and was even able to throw a nice little ceramic set as my final project.

SWC SEMESTER 6

FALL 2025

ART341/ART 344/ART 345/BIOL 317/KOR430

The fall 2025 semester was my first semester at SDSU. This was a big milestone for me as I never really thought I’d transfer from my community college. This semester is where I was able to get into more of the classes that dealt heavy with graphic design but also introduced me to my upper division classes. I learned more about graphic design through my graphic design 2 class and even learned about it’s history in my graphic design history class. I also enrolled in design for the internet 1, which got me to explore and learn more about Figma. The other non-art related classes I took were interesting too. I got to learn about dinosaurs and Korean culture and imperialism through film. In-all my first semester to me felt like a success and I already have nostalgia thinking about it.
